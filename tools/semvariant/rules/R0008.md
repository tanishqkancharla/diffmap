# Give each rule one complete claim and explain its judgment.

Titles appear without bodies in generated guides. Write one complete, specific claim that tells a reader what applies, rather than a topic label or checklist of unrelated obligations.

Bodies are freeform Markdown. Include the rationale, scope, exceptions, evidence, and examples needed to apply the claim. A short paragraph needs no section template. Rules guide judgment; mechanical checks validate structure and projections.
